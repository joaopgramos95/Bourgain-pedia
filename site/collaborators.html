Collaborators

Everyone who wrote a paper with Bourgain, by the first letter of their surname. The collaborator score is the number of joint papers. Blanks are honest blanks: where we could not establish a date or an affiliation, the field is left empty rather than guessed. Dates and the affiliation line come from Wikidata — accepted only when the person could actually have written the joint paper — and the affiliation is the most recently started one on record, which is not always the current one. The prose below it is ours.
