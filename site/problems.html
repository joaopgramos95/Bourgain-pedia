Open problems

Questions left open by the papers, gathered by field. A problem appears here only because we found it posed in a paper we have digested — not because we remember it being open. Each entry records the problem as its source stated it, the year, and what the source itself proves; whether it is still open is a separate field, and stays unchecked until someone verifies it against a source they have read.
